# Bønnetid

## What it is

Consumer prayer times app for Muslims in Norway. Shows when each prayer starts for the user's kommune, jamat and jummah times in the user's own mosque, a hijri calendar with Islamic events, qibla direction, and nearby mosques.

## Who uses it

All ages, from teenagers to grandparents. Used several times a day in quick glances: at home in the morning, at work before duhr, in the car before maghrib. Bright daylight and indoor lighting. The user is in a task ("when is asr?", "when is jummah?"); the interface must answer in under two seconds.

## Register

product

## Platform

adaptive

## Design implications

- Light, calm, high-contrast surfaces. Older users must read times without effort: large tabular numerals, 4.5:1 minimum contrast everywhere.
- No gradients, no decorative effects. Color marks state and identity only.
- Emerald green is the brand tint (Islamic association, bonnetid.no identity). Gold reserved for rare accents.
- Norwegian bokmål UI copy.
- Accuracy is trust: location and mosque selection are explicit, visible settings, never silent guesses.
